BUILD A REAL AI PRODUCT CALLED:

SZTUZK JEWELRY STUDIO
AI JEWELRY DESIGNER

This is a commercial digital product that I intend to sell as a jewelry-maker/business tool through marketplaces such as Etsy and Gumroad.

The current application must NOT be a dummy/demo.

The main purpose is to solve real problems for handmade jewelry makers.

==================================================
CORE CUSTOMER PROBLEM
==================================================

A jewelry maker often has an idea but doesn't know:

- What design to make
- Which materials/components to use
- How many components are required
- How to combine colors and components
- How to make the jewelry
- How much the complete piece will cost
- What selling price to use
- How much profit they may make
- How to reduce cost
- How to create alternative versions
- Whether the design may be too heavy, expensive, complicated, or visually crowded

SZTUZK JEWELRY AI DESIGNER must solve these problems in one workflow.
